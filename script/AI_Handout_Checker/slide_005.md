The pipeline's first stage reads a messy scan, where printed text and pen handwriting mix.
A vision language model called Nanonets transcribes the page, even handwritten verbs inside this printed chart.
Then we tuned the resolution so faint pencil corrections survive without mangling the printed words.
Finally, a computer vision detector finds circled answers, because the language model cannot tell when nothing is circled.
The model just names the circled word, handing clean answers to the grading stage.
