The dataset is built from still pictures, six grabbed every hundred and fifty milliseconds from the borescope video.
Each hole becomes a set of five frames, one at every depth position.
Ten battery modules of twelve holes supply a hundred and twenty good holes.
Sixteen more modules, two for each of eight defect types, supply a hundred and ninety two defective holes.
About eighty percent goes to training, leaving twenty four good and forty defective holes for validation.
