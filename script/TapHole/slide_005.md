Catching those defects starts with data acquisition, imaging inside five and six millimetre holes.
A cheap borescope with a three point nine millimetre probe fits inside, filming at twelve eighty by seven twenty.
First the footage is saved as a video file, then trimmed into frames.
Next, the region of interest is cropped from each frame, so only the threads remain.
Finally, frames are tagged at five probe depths, from entry down to the bottom and back out.
