Defective tap recognition replaces that fixed threshold with a deep learning model.
Its objective is to sort every industrial tap into three classes.
First come normal taps with no defect, then burrs with leftover metal on the thread, and finally broken taps.
For the approach, we chose the YOLO version eight classification model, because its inference is simple and fast.
It also deploys easily on the device itself, once we train it on our own tap images.
