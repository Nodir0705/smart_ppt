Against that thread geometry, tapped holes in the battery case show several distinct defects.
Burrs leave chips between the threads, and a bad chamfer gives the entry bevel the wrong angle.
A broken tap tears threads out, while a double tap cuts twice and spoils the thread surface.
Some holes are never tapped, tapped too shallow, or cut with a tap too small or too big.
The inspection system must catch every one of these from inside the hole.
