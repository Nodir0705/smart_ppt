The problem behind this vision system is tap hole inspection on an automatic battery case inspector.
It must check twelve holes on four sides within fifteen seconds, just over a second each.
The job is confirming each five or six millimetre hole is properly tapped.
A loading jig, two scan units and transfer units carry each case through the machine.
Finally, it must sort eight defect types, such as big, small or short taps, mostly matters of thread geometry.
