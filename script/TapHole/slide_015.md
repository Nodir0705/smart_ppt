To train that YOLO classifier, the defective tap dataset is split eighty to twenty into training and validation.
Normal taps are the largest group, two hundred twenty eight for training and fifty seven for validation.
Burrs are the rarest, with only fifty five training and fourteen validation images.
Broken taps fall in between, at ninety six and twenty four.
The photos show a chipped burr, a clean normal thread, and a damaged broken tap for the model to learn.
