Judging those tapped holes starts with thread standards and definitions.
Pitch is the spacing from one thread crest to the next, measured along the axis.
The major diameter spans the crests, the minor diameter the roots, and the pitch diameter sits between them.
Lead is how far a nut advances per turn, equal to pitch for a single thread.
The metric table lists the standard pitch for every size, our yardstick for the ways a tapped hole fails.
